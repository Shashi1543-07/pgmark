Guided flow — step 3 of 5

Identify a photo

Detect, find the flank, match against the catalogue, and decide — auto-accept, send for review, or enrol as a tiger not seen before. The keypoints behind the match are a fixed geometric guess for now (AUDIT_AND_REVISED_PLAN.md Task 4 replaces this with a trained model), so treat any match here as provisional until that lands.

What makes a good photo

  • Side profile beats any other angle. The stripe pattern that identifies a tiger runs along the flank — a photo taken from the side gives the matcher the most pattern to work with. Head-on and rear shots carry little to no flank and usually cannot be matched.
  • Night infrared is accepted, not yet proven. The pipeline runs the same steps on an IR frame as on a daylight one, but every accuracy figure quoted for this system so far was measured on daylight photography. Treat night-IR matches here as unvalidated rather than assume they perform the same.
  • A crop with no visible flank will be refused, on purpose. If the animal is too close, too far, or obscured such that no clean flank can be extracted, the system declines to match rather than guess — that refusal is deliberate (see the review queue for anything it is unsure about, not this).
